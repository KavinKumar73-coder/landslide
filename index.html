    WHY MACHINE LEARNING?

    Machine learning can combine multiple environmental parameters and classify observed conditions into predefined risk categories based on the training data. In this prototype, a Random Forest model maps rainfall, soil moisture, ground tilt, ground movement and temperature onto a LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk class.

    No specific accuracy figure is claimed here, since this prototype has not undergone field-validated model evaluation.
